Case Study 01 Enterprise SaaS

A calmer signup for high-stakes communication.

Redesigning the LEKAB self-service portal so enterprise users could onboard without abandonment, without losing trust, and without overwhelming admins. A study in progressive disclosure inside a security-first environment.

RoleUI/UX Designer, Lead
ClientLEKAB Communication AB
Timeline4 Weeks[cite: 12]
ToolsFigma, FigJam[cite: 12]
Platforms SupportedCross-platform (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet)[cite: 12]
IndustryEnterprise Messaging & Alerts
Overview

Turning a security-heavy signup into a guided, progressive flow.

LEKAB is an enterprise messaging platform built to broadcast SMS, MMS, and emergency alerts where valid phone numbers and accurate routing are operational necessities[cite: 12]. The onboarding experience had to stay compliant without punishing new users.

Context

Enterprise users were asked for excessive data upfront before seeing value, causing high abandonment rates and falsified fields[cite: 12].

My Role

End-to-end ownership: qualitative research with 15 users[cite: 13], journey mapping, information architecture, prototyping, and testing[cite: 12].

Impact

Lowered initial registration drop-offs, improved data accuracy, and eliminated administrative bottlenecks[cite: 17].

The problem

Too much, too early, too unclear.

"Users frequently abandon the signup process when asked to provide too much information upfront, particularly before understanding the platform's value[cite: 12]."

~40%

Drop-off before completion on legacy signup flows.

Bad Data

Inaccurate phone data broke campaign delivery and blocked OTPs[cite: 12].

Manual

Legacy accounts required admin intervention for access codes.

Support

High support ticket load tied to incomplete registrations.

Research & insights

Listening before we redesigned anything.

Conducted user interviews with 15 users across different companies and departments, mapping current journeys alongside support and leveraging form-conversion research to shape our four core personas[cite: 13].

MA

Marcus

Enterprise IT Administrator
Goals

Securely provision department access using trusted enterprise credentials (SSO) and reCAPTCHA[cite: 13].

Frustrations

Rigid legacy forms demanding excessive hierarchy data and unverified phone numbers upfront[cite: 13].

Behavior

Demands clean decoupled authentication flows and automated compliance tracking.

"As an IT Administrator, I want a secure authentication gateway with email verification, SSO, and spam protection so that I can safely provision access for my department[cite: 13]."
SO

Sofia

Marketing Campaign Manager
Goals

Access the messaging portal instantly and input valid mobile routing parameters for bulk campaigns[cite: 14].

Frustrations

Cumbersome login walls when scheduling time-sensitive flash-sale broadcasts[cite: 14].

Behavior

Rushes through bad forms if blocked, breaking delivery metrics and OTPs[cite: 14].

"As a Marketing Manager, I want a frictionless initial login so that I can quickly access the portal without administrative delays[cite: 14]."
DA

David

Customer Support Lead
Goals

Manage emergency incident alerts reliably across desktop and mobile browsers (app.lekab.com)[cite: 14].

Frustrations

Portal lag or profile lockouts during high-stress operational crises and outages[cite: 14].

Behavior

Requires uninterrupted communication channels tied to accurate contact numbers on the go[cite: 14].

"As a Support Lead, I want reliable access across browsers so that I can manage emergency incident alerts without broken notification chains[cite: 14]."
EL

Elena

Finance & Billing Manager
Goals

Ensure enterprise accounts are correctly linked to verified contact numbers and clear department attributes[cite: 15].

Frustrations

Behavior

"As a Finance Manager, I want a clear profile structure ensuring our enterprise account is tied to verified contact numbers and correct department attributes[cite: 15]."
