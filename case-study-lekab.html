Case Study 01 Enterprise SaaS

A calmer signup for high-stakes communication.

Redesigning the LEKAB self-service portal so enterprise users could onboard without abandonment, without losing trust, and without overwhelming admins. A study in progressive disclosure inside a security-first environment.

RoleUI/UX Designer, Lead
ClientLEKAB Communication AB
Timeline4 Weeks[cite: 12]
ToolsFigma, FigJam[cite: 12]
Platforms SupportedCross-platform (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet)[cite: 12]
IndustryEnterprise Messaging & Alerts
Overview

Turning a security-heavy signup into a guided, progressive flow.

LEKAB is an enterprise messaging platform built to broadcast SMS, MMS, and emergency alerts where valid phone numbers and accurate routing are operational necessities[cite: 12]. The onboarding experience had to stay compliant without punishing new users.

Context

Enterprise users were asked for excessive data upfront before seeing value, causing high abandonment rates and falsified fields[cite: 12].

My Role

End-to-end ownership: qualitative research with 15 users[cite: 13], journey mapping, information architecture, prototyping, and testing[cite: 12].

Impact

Lowered initial registration drop-offs, improved data accuracy, and eliminated administrative bottlenecks[cite: 17].

The problem

Too much, too early, too unclear.

"Users frequently abandon the signup process when asked to provide too much information upfront, particularly before understanding the platform's value[cite: 12]."

~40%

Drop-off before completion on legacy signup flows.

Bad Data

Inaccurate phone data broke campaign delivery and blocked OTPs[cite: 12].

Manual

Research & personas

Listening before we redesigned anything.

Conducted user interviews with 15 users across different companies and departments, mapping current journeys alongside support and establishing four comprehensive enterprise personas and user stories[cite: 61, 62].

Marcus

Enterprise IT Administrator
Focus: Security & Compliance[cite: 62]
Scope: Mid-sized Enterprise[cite: 62]
Archetype: Gatekeeper & Provisioner
diligent skeptical compliance-driven structured
"Security should be robust without creating operational roadblocks."
Background

Marcus manages security compliance, user provisioning, and authentication protocols for a mid-sized enterprise. He lives by strict security audits and is responsible for ensuring that only authorized personnel gain access to company communication channels[cite: 62].

What He Wants to Do

Securely provision department access using trusted enterprise credentials (such as Google, Microsoft, or Apple SSO) and maintain strict reCAPTCHA spam protection without creating administrative roadblocks for his team[cite: 62].

In-Depth Pain Points
  • Deeply frustrated by legacy enterprise software that uses rigid, bloated registration forms compared to modern developer tools (AWS, GitHub, Vercel)[cite: 62].
  • Excessive upfront data demands (corporate hierarchies, billing codes, unverified personal numbers) cause annoyance, field skipping, and falsified data, creating severe security blind spots[cite: 62].
What He Suggests / Expects

A clean, decoupled authentication flow where basic identity is confirmed instantly, allowing administrators and team members to enter the platform safely and complete secondary compliance data later through progressive prompts[cite: 62].

User Story: "As an IT Administrator, I want a secure authentication gateway with email verification, SSO, and spam protection so that I can safely provision access for my department without users abandoning the form or submitting fake credentials[cite: 62]."

Sofia

Marketing Campaign Manager
Focus: Bulk Campaigns & Speed[cite: 63]
Scope: National Retail Brand[cite: 63]
Archetype: Fast-Paced Executioner
energetic results-oriented impatient with friction agile
"Timing is everything when scheduling flash-sale broadcasts."
Background

Sofia coordinates high-volume bulk SMS, MMS, and RCS promotional campaigns across multiple channels for a national retail brand. She operates in a fast-paced environment where timing is everything[cite: 62, 63].

What She Wants to Do

Access the messaging portal instantly, create broadcast workflows, and input valid mobile routing parameters without administrative bottlenecks[cite: 63].

In-Depth Pain Points
  • Detests cumbersome, outdated login walls when needing immediate access for time-sensitive flash-sale broadcasts (compares to Mailchimp, HubSpot)[cite: 63].
  • Long upfront forms caused high drop-offs; users rushed through and entered incorrect or fake phone numbers, sabotaging delivery metrics, blocking OTPs, and ruining analytics[cite: 63].
What She Suggests / Expects

A frictionless initial login that gets her straight to her dashboard, with intelligent, contextual prompts that only ask for specialized campaign or department attributes when setting up a live broadcast[cite: 63].

User Story: "As a Marketing Manager, I want a frictionless initial login so that I can quickly access the portal and input valid mobile routing parameters for bulk SMS/RCS campaigns without administrative delays[cite: 63]."

David

Customer Support Lead
Focus: Incident Response & Outages[cite: 63]
Scope: Emergency Support[cite: 63]
Archetype: Crisis Responder
calm under pressure reliant action-driven transparent
"Uninterrupted communication during critical outages is non-negotiable."
Background

David oversees urgent customer incident alerts, staffing schedules, and two-way customer support workflows. His team relies on uninterrupted, instantaneous communication channels during critical outages[cite: 63].

What He Wants to Do

Log in seamlessly across desktop or mobile browsers to manage emergency incident alerts and ensure two-way SMS notifications are tied to accurate, working contact numbers on the go[cite: 63].

In-Depth Pain Points
  • Deals with high-stress operational crises; if a portal lags, demands excessive paperwork, or locks him out during an outage, customer satisfaction plummets (compares to PagerDuty, Slack)[cite: 63].
  • Upfront data demands lead to fragmented contact records (missing work vs. private numbers), breaking automated notification chains during emergencies[cite: 63].
What He Suggests / Expects

Immediate dashboard access upon verification, with clear, non-intrusive reminders to update profile details later rather than locking him out or blocking his view during a crisis[cite: 63].

User Story: "As a Support Lead, I want reliable access across desktop and mobile browsers so that I can manage emergency incident alerts and ensure two-way SMS notifications are tied to accurate contact numbers on the go[cite: 63]."

Elena

Finance & Billing Manager
Focus: Data Accuracy & Invoices[cite: 64]
Scope: Enterprise Billing[cite: 64]
Archetype: Financial Auditor
meticulous analytical budget-conscious precise
"Every euro spent must map correctly to the right department."
Background

Elena monitors operational expenditures, API usage metrics, campaign delivery costs, and monthly communication invoices. She is meticulous about data accuracy and ensures correct department attribution[cite: 63, 64].

What She Wants to Do

Ensure enterprise accounts are correctly linked to verified contact numbers and clear department attributes so billing history, account balances, and usage metrics match accurately[cite: 64].

In-Depth Pain Points
  • Suffers from massive end-of-month reconciliation errors caused by bad data captured during flawed registration processes (compares to Stripe, NetSuite)[cite: 64].
  • Premature department and billing guesses lead to placeholder data, unverified numbers, and misattributed department codes, turning monthly auditing into a nightmare[cite: 64].
What She Suggests / Expects

Contextual task gating where secondary billing and department attributes are prompted precisely when the user attempts to access financial modules or run billable campaigns[cite: 64].

User Story: "As a Finance Manager, I want a clear profile structure ensuring our enterprise account is tied to verified contact numbers and correct department attributes for accurate billing tracking[cite: 64]."
