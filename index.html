✦ Featured Case Study

Designing for secure, high-stakes communication workflows.

LEKAB is a communication and workflow automation platform used across healthcare, finance, logistics, public sector, retail, and manufacturing. The work centered on reducing friction, increasing trust, and making complex flows feel calm and structured.

8 years experience 12 step flow 1 platform redesign
LEKAB AB End-to-end UX ownership across research, architecture, design, validation, and handoff for sandbox and production portals.
01 Problem

Users were blocked by manual access and unclear data requirements.

Users had to contact an admin for an ID and access code, and many abandoned the flow when too much information was requested upfront. Missing or incorrect data created risk, support overhead, and poor trust.

WhoEnterprise users, admins, and support teams.
PainAbandonment, missing data, and operational friction.
NeedA clearer, safer, and more progressive onboarding experience.
RiskIncorrect data affected security and personalization.
SupportAdmin workload increased because of manual steps.
TrustThe upfront flow felt heavy and unclear.
Drop-offMany users abandoned the process early.
02 Objective

Make signup feel guided, safe, and easy to complete.

The goal was to implement a user-friendly signup flow that reduces abandonment while collecting accurate information gradually. The experience had to explain why details matter without compromising security.

GoalReduce abandonment while improving data quality.
ToneClear, reassuring, and high trust.
ConstraintSecurity and compliance remained non-negotiable.
ExplainWhy each field is required.
ProgressiveAsk for data step by step.
MinimizeAdmin involvement and confusion.
IncreaseAccuracy and completeness of records.
03 Approach

Map the workflow, remove friction, and validate early.

User research identified the key friction points, so the flow was redesigned as a phased signup with minimal upfront input. Wireframes and prototypes were developed collaboratively with engineers and product managers with a strong focus on accessibility, responsiveness, and visual consistency.

Discovery and Research

Led user research to identify bottlenecks, risky steps, and places where users lost confidence.

Workflow Architecture

Mapped the end-to-end logic and reorganized information hierarchy to keep each step focused.

Prototype and Test

Built high-fidelity prototypes and validated them with usability checks before handoff.

04 Solution

A phased signup flow with clearer explanations and less stress.

The solution introduced a phased signup flow to reduce friction, with clear explanations for why specific information is required. Notifications and admin processes were streamlined to improve efficiency, and the flow was validated through usability testing to ensure smooth completion and accurate data collection.

What Changed

Users could provide data at the right moment instead of being overwhelmed at the start.

System Impact

Admin processes were simplified, and the platform felt more mature and dependable.

Design Outcome

The experience became more readable, responsive, and consistent across screens.

05 Impact

Measured outcomes and business value.

Impact Summary

The updated flow reduced signup abandonment, improved the accuracy and completeness of user information, increased user trust and satisfaction, and lowered the administrative workload required for account creation.

Business Value

The redesign reduced friction in a high-stakes environment while strengthening the platform’s credibility and operational efficiency.

06 12-Step Flow

End-to-end design process

User interviews, workflow observations, and bottleneck analysis.

02 Synthesis and insights

Patterns, risks, and opportunities grouped into themes.

Clear problem statements aligned with business goals and constraints.

04 Workflow and structure

Redundant steps removed and decision points clarified.

05 Information architecture

Content hierarchy simplified and labels clarified.

06 Wireframing

07 Interactive prototyping

08 UI design

09 Design system alignment

Reusable components and patterns kept the product scalable.

10 Validation and testing

Direct user sessions checked trust, clarity, and behavior.

11 Iteration

Feedback was applied to reduce friction and visual noise.

12 Handoff and delivery

Final designs, prototypes, and implementation notes were prepared for engineers.
