Designing for Secure Communication Workflows

LEKAB Communication Systems AB provides secure communication and workflow automation for healthcare, finance, logistics, public sector, retail, and manufacturing. The redesign focused on making a complex B2B platform more usable, reliable, and scalable.

Problem

Users had to contact an admin for access codes, key data was often incomplete or wrong, and the experience created abandonment, trust issues, and extra operational work.

Objective

Create a user-friendly onboarding flow that gradually collects accurate data, reduces abandonment, and explains the value of providing each detail.

Platform Context
  • Sandbox: safe testing space for messaging APIs and workflow automation.
  • App.LEKAB: live production portal for real-time messaging and mission-critical workflows.
  • Channels: SMS, voice, email, mobile app, WhatsApp, alerts, and OTP flows.
HealthcareCritical coordination and workforce communication.
FinanceSecure onboarding and OTP delivery.

⚙ Delivery Ownership

Workflow Architecture, UI Design, and Handoff

I led discovery, mapped workflows, designed high-fidelity interfaces, and aligned the design system so the product could scale with consistency and less implementation friction.

Process
  • Discovery and research to identify bottlenecks, high-risk actions, and usability failures.
  • Workflow architecture to define hierarchy and simplify decision points.
  • High-fidelity design with accessible, responsive, and clean UI components.
  • Design system alignment to reduce debt and support engineering speed.
Key Flows
  • Account signup and login with phased onboarding.
  • Password reset with email, confirmation code, and new password steps.
  • Validation and usability testing before handoff.
  • Iteration based on trust, clarity, and error reduction.
